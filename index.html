How the numbers are computed

  • Price rule: only sales from $10,000 to $5,000,000 are included. The dataset has a few impossible prices (one at $0, one at $81.5 million), and this rule leaves them out.
  • Days on market is contract date minus list date, counted only on sales that have both dates. A blank date is never treated as zero.
  • Price per sq ft skips homes whose living area is blank or 0.
  • Medians are used instead of averages because a few very expensive homes would pull an average up.
  • Distance is straight-line miles to each campus's landmark, from the miles_to_campus column.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
